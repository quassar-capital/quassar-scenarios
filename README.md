# Quassar Scenarios

**Macro-Financial Scenario Analysis | Cross-Asset Stress Research | Quantitative Simulation | Economic Risk Modeling**

## Overview

Quassar Scenarios is a quantitative research and computational engineering initiative within Quassar Capital, focused on the mathematical representation and analysis of macroeconomic conditions, financial market shocks, and cross-asset interactions.

Its research scope encompasses economic scenario construction, financial sensitivity analysis, stochastic modeling, and the examination of market behavior under alternative economic and financial conditions.

The methodological framework emphasizes economic coherence, statistical rigor, numerical reliability, transparent assumptions, and reproducibility.

## Research Domains

### Macroeconomic Scenario Analysis

Quantitative methods for examining relationships among economic growth, inflation, monetary policy, interest rates, employment, and financial conditions.

Research topics include macroeconomic transmission mechanisms, alternative economic environments, parameter sensitivity, and the consistency of assumptions across interconnected economic variables.

### Cross-Asset Scenario Modeling

Mathematical approaches to evaluating relationships among equities, fixed income, foreign exchange, commodities, credit markets, and volatility.

Areas of interest include cross-asset dependencies, correlation instability, nonlinear sensitivities, market regime transitions, and the propagation of common economic shocks.

### Financial Stress Scenarios

Analytical methods for examining financial market behavior under adverse hypothetical conditions and historically observed disruptions.

Research encompasses interest rate shocks, credit spread widening, liquidity deterioration, equity market declines, volatility changes, and combinations of simultaneous market disturbances.

### Stochastic Scenario Generation

Probability-based approaches to representing uncertainty in economic and financial variables.

Research topics include stochastic processes, Monte Carlo simulation, multivariate distributions, dependence structures, parameter estimation, and the statistical properties of simulated outcomes.

### Market Regimes & Structural Change

Quantitative methods for studying changes in economic and financial relationships across market environments.

Areas of interest include regime classification, structural breaks, time-varying volatility, changing correlations, and the stability of statistical relationships under different economic and financial conditions.

### Scenario Sensitivity & Uncertainty Analysis

Mathematical approaches to evaluating how analytical outcomes respond to changes in assumptions, parameters, model specifications, and underlying economic conditions.

Research emphasizes sensitivity decomposition, uncertainty quantification, robustness analysis, and the identification of material sources of estimation and model risk.

## Quantitative Research Methodology

The quantitative research methodology is organized around the following principles:

- **Economic Coherence:** Clearly defined assumptions and internally consistent relationships among economic and financial variables.
- **Mathematical Rigor:** Explicit model formulations, well-defined parameters, consistent notation, and appropriate analytical methods.
- **Statistical Reliability:** Sound estimation procedures, diagnostic testing, uncertainty assessment, and recognition of finite-sample limitations.
- **Numerical Verification:** Evaluation of computational accuracy, convergence, stability, and numerical approximation error.
- **Scenario Consistency:** Transparent treatment of dependencies, conditional assumptions, and relationships among simulated variables.
- **Stochastic Integrity:** Appropriate treatment of probability distributions, dependence structures, random sampling, and simulation uncertainty.
- **Reproducibility:** Documented computational procedures, controlled dependencies, traceable assumptions, and repeatable analytical experiments.
- **Model Validation:** Benchmark comparisons, historical evaluation, sensitivity testing, and explicit assessment of model limitations.
- **Responsible Research:** Protection of confidential financial information, proprietary methodologies, and restricted datasets.

## Technology & Computational Infrastructure

The computational scope encompasses programming languages and numerical technologies relevant to macroeconomic modeling, stochastic simulation, financial mathematics, and scientific computing. Technology selection is determined by the analytical requirements of each application rather than by a prescribed implementation stack.

Relevant technologies include:

- **Python:** Statistical modeling, scenario analysis, numerical simulation, data processing, and quantitative research.
- **C++:** Performance-sensitive numerical algorithms, computational kernels, and simulation-intensive applications.
- **Rust:** Systems programming, memory-safe computational components, and reliable software architecture.
- **Julia:** Mathematical modeling, scientific computing, stochastic simulation, and numerical optimization.
- **GPU-Accelerated Computing:** Parallel simulation, matrix-intensive computation, and computationally demanding statistical workloads where technically justified.

Core engineering principles include modular architecture, version control, automated testing, numerical regression analysis, benchmark evaluation, scientific documentation, and reproducible computational environments.

## Research Governance & Model Integrity

The research governance framework is defined by methodological transparency, economic consistency, statistical discipline, information security, and responsible handling of financial research materials.

### Research Integrity

Economic assumptions, mathematical formulations, computational procedures, and analytical conclusions require accurate representation, with appropriate consideration of uncertainty and methodological limitations.

### Model Verification & Validation

Scenario models are evaluated according to suitable numerical verification methods, statistical diagnostics, sensitivity analysis, benchmark comparisons, and validation criteria relevant to their intended analytical applications.

### Scenario Interpretation

Hypothetical scenarios are distinguished from forecasts, observed market outcomes, and statements of expected financial performance.

Scenario results are conditional on their underlying assumptions, probability models, estimation procedures, and mathematical specifications. The plausibility of a scenario does not establish its probability of occurrence.

### Statistical Robustness

Research interpretation accounts for estimation uncertainty, model specification risk, dependence assumptions, parameter instability, and the limitations of historical observations as representations of future market conditions.

### Data Governance

Research data is subject to appropriate quality controls, provenance requirements, confidentiality considerations, and access restrictions.

### Information Security

Proprietary investment models, confidential portfolio exposures, internal risk parameters, restricted datasets, and production financial infrastructure are excluded from public research materials.

### Intellectual Property

Public research materials are distinguished from proprietary methodologies, confidential information, and intellectual property belonging to Quassar Capital or third parties.

## Scope of Public Materials

Public disclosure of research materials is subject to technical, institutional, intellectual property, and information security review.

Materials eligible for public distribution may include general-purpose scenario methodologies, nonproprietary economic simulations, reproducible numerical examples, academic research software, and technical documentation.

Confidential investment assumptions, proprietary portfolio exposures, internal risk parameters, restricted datasets, trading signals, and proprietary financial infrastructure are excluded from public disclosure.
